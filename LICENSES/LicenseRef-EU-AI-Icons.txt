EU Icons for labelling AI-generated content

These icons are made publicly available for everyone to use freely, without
the need for attribution to the Commission or the AI Office.

Signatories of the code of practice must follow the placement specifications
in Section 2 of the Code of Practice on marking and labelling of AI-generated
content. Usage of these icons by non-signatories of the code should not be
construed as signaling of their adherence to the code.
